How it works

1. Tell us your goal

Race, date, the training you do now and the hours you really have. Your plan is built backwards from race day.

2. Safety first

A short health screen runs before anything is built. If something needs a doctor's all-clear, we say so instead of guessing.

3. Review, then train

You review your whole plan, adjust it if your week changes, then it lands in the Coachly app on your phone and watch.

Written by a coach's method, not a template

Plans follow the periodisation Ollie Allan uses with Tri Alliance squads in Melbourne: build weeks with a lighter week every third week, long sessions that grow toward race day and a proper taper. Sessions come from the Tri Alliance library of proven workouts, each written out with a warm up, main set and cool down.
